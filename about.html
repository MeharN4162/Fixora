About Fixora

Fixora was built around a simple idea: everyday tech tasks — compressing a file, resizing a photo, generating a password — shouldn't require an account, a download, or a subscription. They should just work, instantly, for free.

What We Build

We build two things: lightweight, browser-based utilities that solve one specific problem really well, and clear, practical how-to guides that pair with those tools. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser — files you upload are never sent to a server, which means faster results and better privacy by default.

How Our Tools Work

Modern browsers are powerful enough to handle image processing, file conversion, and text manipulation without needing a backend server. We take advantage of that: when you use our PDF Compressor, Image Resizer, or Password Generator, all of the processing happens locally on your device using standard web technologies.

Why It's Free

Fixora is supported by minimal, non-intrusive advertising, which lets us keep every tool free with no usage limits and no account requirement. We don't sell your data — because we never receive it in the first place.

Our Commitment

  • No sign-up or account required for any tool
  • No file uploads to external servers — everything processes in your browser
  • No artificial usage limits or paywalls on core functionality
  • Clear, jargon-free guides written to actually solve your problem

Have a tool idea or found something that doesn't work as expected? We'd love to hear from you on our Contact page.
