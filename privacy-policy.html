Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fixoona ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you visit fixoona.com (the "Site"). We built this Site around a simple principle: process as little of your data as possible, and never touch the files you work with.

1. Files You Process With Our Tools

Every tool on this Site — including the PDF Compressor, PDF to JPG converter, Image Resizer, JPG to PNG converter, Word Counter, Password Generator, Text Case Converter, and Random Name Generator — runs entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript. Files you upload or text you type are never transmitted to our servers or any third party. Everything is processed locally on your device and discarded when you close or refresh the page.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

Like most websites, our web hosting provider and any analytics tools we use may automatically collect standard technical information, such as:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on the Site
  • Referring website or search engine

This information is used only in aggregate to understand how the Site is used and to improve performance and content.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies or similar local storage technologies to remember basic preferences and to support advertising and analytics services described below. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; doing so will not affect your ability to use our tools.

4. Advertising

This Site may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad preferences at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate visitor behavior. These services may set their own cookies and collect data as described in their respective privacy policies.

6. Contact Form

If you use our Contact page, any information you submit (name, email, and message) is sent directly to us via your own email client and used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to a mailing list without your consent.

7. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Choices

  • You can disable cookies through your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ad Settings.
  • You can contact us at any time to ask what information, if any, we hold about you.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page or email meharnaangal@gmail.com.
