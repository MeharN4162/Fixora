Count business days between two dates, or add business days to a date — skipping weekends and holidays.

How to Use the Business Days Calculator

  1. Optionally list any holidays to skip, one per line.
  2. Enter a start and end date to count the business days between them.
  3. Or enter a date and a number of business days to add, to find the resulting date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which days count as weekends?

This tool treats Saturday and Sunday as non-business days, which is the standard Monday-to-Friday work week used in most countries. It doesn't account for countries with a different weekend (like Friday–Saturday).

How do I exclude holidays from the calculation?

Add each holiday date on its own line in the holidays box, in YYYY-MM-DD format. Any date listed there is skipped in both the counting and the add-days calculation, just like a weekend.

Is the start date itself counted?

Yes — when counting business days between two dates, both the start and end dates are included in the count if they themselves fall on a business day.

Are the dates I enter sent anywhere?

No — all the date math happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you enter is ever sent to a server.
