Get a random clean, corny dad joke with one click.

How to Use the Dad Joke Generator

  1. Click "Another One" for a fresh random dad joke anytime.
  2. Click "Copy Joke" to copy it to share.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do these jokes come from?

They're a hand-picked, fixed collection of classic dad jokes built directly into this page — not pulled from a live external API.

Are these jokes appropriate for kids?

Yes — every joke in the collection is clean, family-friendly wordplay, the classic "dad joke" style of pun-based humor with no profanity or adult content.

Will I see the same joke twice in a row?

The generator avoids repeating the exact joke you just saw, so clicking "Another One" reliably gives you something different each time.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — joke selection happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing is ever sent to a server.
