Number to Words Converter

Convert a number into English words, including a check-writing currency format.

How to Convert a Number to Words

  1. Type a number.
  2. Optionally check "Format as currency" for a check-writing style output.
  3. Copy the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the currency format used for?

It's the standard format used when writing a paper check by hand, like "One thousand two hundred thirty-four and 56/100 dollars," which spells out the dollar amount in words and writes the cents as a fraction.

What's the largest number this tool can convert?

It supports whole numbers up to 999,999,999,999 (just under a trillion). Numbers larger than that aren't supported since they're far beyond what a check-writing or everyday spelled-out-number use case would need.

Does it handle negative numbers?

Yes — a negative number is converted the same way, with the word "negative" placed in front of the spelled-out value.

Is the number I enter sent anywhere?

No — the conversion happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you type is ever sent to a server.
