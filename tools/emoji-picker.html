Emoji Picker

Search hundreds of emoji by name or keyword and click any of them to copy it straight to your clipboard.

No emoji matched your search. Try a different word.

How to Find and Copy an Emoji

  1. Type a word into the search box — like "dog", "heart", or "pizza" — to filter the grid by name or keyword.
  2. Or click a category pill (Smileys, Animals, Food, and more) to browse a specific group.
  3. Click any emoji in the grid to copy it to your clipboard.
  4. Paste it wherever you need it — a chat, a document, a caption, anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many emoji are included in this picker?

This picker includes a curated set of over 250 commonly used emoji across categories like smileys, animals, food, activities, travel, objects, symbols, and hearts — covering the emoji people search for and use most often.

How does clicking an emoji copy it?

Clicking any emoji button copies that exact character to your clipboard using your browser's Clipboard API, so you can paste it straight into a message, document, or post.

Why doesn't a particular emoji show up in my search?

Search matches against each emoji's name and a short list of keywords, so if a search term isn't covered, try a more common word for what you're looking for — the emoji itself might use a different official name than you expect.

Will the emoji look the same on every device?

No — the underlying character is identical everywhere, but its exact appearance depends on your device and app's emoji font, so the same emoji can look a little different on, say, an iPhone versus a Windows PC.

Do I need to install anything to use this?

No — everything runs directly in this page using your browser's built-in emoji rendering and Clipboard API; there's nothing to download or install.
