How to Split or Remove Pages from a PDF

  1. Choose a mode: "Remove Pages" (keep everything except what you click) or "Extract Pages" (keep only what you click) using the pill buttons at the top.
  2. Upload your PDF — every page renders as a clickable thumbnail.
  3. Click the pages you want to mark (✕ for removal, ✓ for extraction) — or use "Mark All" / "Clear Marks" to speed this up.
  4. Click "Remove Selected Pages" or "Extract Selected Pages" and download the resulting PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Remove Pages and Extract Pages mode?

In Remove Pages mode, the pages you click are deleted and every unmarked page is kept. In Extract Pages mode, it's the opposite — only the pages you click are kept, and everything else is discarded. Switching modes clears your current selection so you don't accidentally mix the two up.

Why can't I remove all the pages?

In Remove Pages mode, the button is disabled if you've marked every single page, since that would leave a PDF with zero pages. If you want a single page as your output, switch to Extract Pages mode and mark just that one page instead.

Do the extracted or edited pages keep their original quality?

Yes. Pages are copied directly from the source PDF's internal structure using pdf-lib, not re-rendered as images, so text stays selectable and there's no quality loss.

What happens to the page order?

The order is preserved. Whether you're removing or extracting, the remaining pages keep the same relative order they had in the original document.

Can I select pages from anywhere in a long document?

Yes — every page in the PDF is rendered as a thumbnail up front, so you can scroll through and click pages in any order, not just consecutive ranges.
