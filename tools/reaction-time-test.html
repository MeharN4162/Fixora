How to Test Your Reaction Time

  1. Click the box to start.
  2. Wait for it to turn from red to green.
  3. Click as fast as you can the moment it turns green.
  4. Repeat to track your best and average time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the reaction time measured?

The moment the box turns green is recorded using the browser's high-precision performance timer, and the moment you click is recorded the same way — the difference between the two, in milliseconds, is your reaction time.

What counts as a good reaction time?

Most people fall somewhere between 200 and 300 milliseconds for a simple visual reaction test like this one. Under 200ms is unusually fast, and results naturally vary run to run based on focus and screen refresh timing.

What happens if I click before the box turns green?

It's counted as "too soon" and doesn't record a time, since clicking before the cue appears isn't a real reaction — it just resets so you can try again.

Is my score saved or sent anywhere?

No — your best and average times are only kept in memory for your current visit, and everything happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server or saved after you leave the page.
