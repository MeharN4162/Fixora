Photo Collage Maker

Upload 2 to 6 photos, pick a grid layout, and download a single combined collage image.

🖼️

How to Make a Photo Collage

  1. Click the upload area or drag in 2 to 6 JPG, PNG, or WebP photos.
  2. Press and hold a thumbnail and drag it to reorder your photos — their order decides which grid cell each one lands in.
  3. Pick a grid layout — only layouts that fit your current number of photos are enabled.
  4. Adjust the gap width and color between photos if you'd like a border.
  5. Click "Generate Collage" to see the result, then "Download Collage" to save it as a PNG.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photos can I use in one collage?

Between 2 and 6. Upload the photos you want, and the layout buttons that need more photos than you've uploaded stay disabled until you add enough - for example, the 6-photo grid only becomes available once you've uploaded at least 6 images.

Will my photos look stretched or distorted in the collage?

No. Each photo is center-cropped to match its grid cell's proportions before being drawn, the same way CSS object-fit: cover works, so photos fill their cell completely without being squashed or stretched out of shape.

Can I control the spacing and border color between photos?

Yes - use the gap slider to set the spacing in pixels between and around photos, and the color picker to set that gap's color, from white borders to a solid black frame or any color you like.

Can I remove a photo after uploading it?

Yes, click the × button on any thumbnail to remove it before generating your collage.

Can I reorder my photos before generating the collage?

Yes — press and hold any thumbnail and drag it to a new position; the thumbnail order determines which grid cell each photo fills once you generate the collage. Small up and down buttons on each thumbnail offer a non-drag way to reorder too.

What file format is the collage download?

A PNG file, exported directly from the output canvas at the layout's full resolution.
