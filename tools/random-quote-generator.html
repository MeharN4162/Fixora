Random Quote Generator

Get a random motivational, wisdom, or funny quote.

How to Use the Random Quote Generator

  1. Optionally pick a category — Motivational, Wisdom, or Funny.
  2. Click "New Quote" for a fresh random quote anytime.
  3. Click "Copy Quote" to copy it with its author.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do these quotes come from?

They're a hand-picked, fixed collection of well-known quotes built directly into this page, organized into Motivational, Wisdom, and Funny categories — not pulled from a live external API.

Can I get a quote from a specific category?

Yes — use the category buttons to restrict random quotes to Motivational, Wisdom, or Funny, or leave it on "All" to draw from every category.

Will I see the same quote twice in a row?

The generator avoids repeating the exact same quote you just saw (as long as more than one quote is available in the current category), so clicking "New Quote" reliably gives you something different each time.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — quote selection happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing is ever sent to a server.
