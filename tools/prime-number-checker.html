Frequently Asked Questions

How does the checker actually verify primality?

It uses trial division, testing whether any whole number from 2 up to the square root of your number divides it evenly — if none do, the number is prime. This is a genuinely correct method, not an approximation, though it becomes slower for extremely large numbers.

Are 0 and 1 prime numbers?

No — by definition, a prime number must be greater than 1. Neither 0 nor 1 counts as prime or composite; 1 is a special case sometimes called a "unit."

How high can the prime list generator go?

It's capped at 1,000,000 to keep the calculation fast and avoid generating an unreasonably long list for your browser to render.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — all calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you enter is ever sent to a server.
