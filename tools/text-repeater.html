Text Repeater

Repeat any word, phrase, or line of text a set number of times.

How to Repeat Text

  1. Type the text you want to repeat.
  2. Set how many times and pick a separator.
  3. Copy the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What separators can I use between repeats?

Any text you like — a space, a comma, a new line, or nothing at all if you want the repeats joined directly together with no separator.

Is there a limit to how many times I can repeat something?

It's capped at 10,000 repeats to keep the browser responsive and avoid generating an unreasonably large block of text that could slow down your device.

What is this actually useful for?

Generating placeholder or stress-test data, creating a repeated pattern for design mockups, or just quickly producing a block of repeated text without manually copying and pasting.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No — the repetition happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you type is ever sent to a server.
