How to Use the Habit Tracker

  1. Add a habit you want to track.
  2. Click any of the last 7 days to mark it complete.
  3. Your current streak updates automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the streak calculated?

It counts consecutive days, working backward from today, that are marked complete. As soon as a day is found that isn't checked off, the streak count stops there.

Can I mark a past day as complete if I forgot to check in?

Yes — the last 7 days are all clickable, not just today, so you can retroactively fill in a day you missed logging.

Where is my habit data stored?

In your browser's local storage on your own device — not on a server or account. It will still be there next time you visit in the same browser, but won't sync to another device.

Is my data ever sent to a server?

No — everything is stored and managed entirely in your browser using JavaScript and local storage.
