Text to Emoji Translator

Turn everyday words in your text into matching emoji.

How to Translate Text to Emoji

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Any word matching the built-in dictionary is instantly replaced with its emoji.
  3. Copy the result with the "Copy Result" button.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it translate every word into an emoji?

No — only words that match its built-in dictionary of a few hundred common everyday words (animals, food, feelings, weather, objects, and more) get replaced. Any word not in the dictionary is left exactly as you typed it.

Is the matching case-sensitive?

No — "Pizza," "pizza," and "PIZZA" all match the same dictionary entry, so capitalization in your original text doesn't affect whether a word gets translated.

Does it match parts of words, like "cat" inside "category"?

No — it only matches whole words, so "category" stays as plain text even though it contains "cat," avoiding the kind of accidental partial-word replacements that would otherwise mangle unrelated words.

Does it handle plurals, like "babies" or "cats"?

Yes — plural forms (including irregular ones like "mice" or "wolves") are recognized and matched to the same emoji as their singular form, so "baby" and "babies" both become 👶.

What happens if I write a number before a word, like "3 cars"?

The emoji is repeated that many times instead of appearing once — "3 cars" becomes 🚗🚗🚗. This is capped at 20 repeats per number to keep the output readable, and "0 (word)" removes the word entirely rather than showing a misleading single emoji for a zero count.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No — the translation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so your text is never sent to a server.
