Frequently Asked Questions

How is pace different from speed?

Speed is distance covered per unit of time (like miles per hour), while pace is the inverse — time taken per unit of distance (like minutes per mile). Runners typically think in pace since it directly answers "how long will each mile take."

Can I predict my finish time for a race distance using my pace?

Yes — enter your pace and the target race distance in the second section, and it multiplies your per-mile or per-kilometer pace by the total distance to estimate a finish time.

Does this account for pacing changes during a race, like fatigue?

No — this assumes a perfectly even pace throughout, which is a simplification. Real race performance often varies due to terrain, fatigue, and pacing strategy, so treat the prediction as a rough estimate rather than a guarantee.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — all calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you enter is ever sent to a server.
