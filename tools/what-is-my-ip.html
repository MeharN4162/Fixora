How This Works

  1. The moment you load this page, your browser asks a public IP-lookup service what address your request is coming from.
  2. That address is displayed above — it's exactly what any website you visit can see.
  3. Everything else on this page (browser, screen, time zone) is read locally from your own browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does this IP address actually come from?

Fixoona has no backend server of its own, so this page asks a well-known public IP-lookup service (ipify.org) directly from your browser, and that service reports back the IP address it sees your request coming from — the same way any website you visit sees your IP.

Is this my exact private home IP address?

No — this is your public IP address, the one your router or ISP presents to the outside internet. If multiple devices share one internet connection (like everyone on the same home Wi-Fi), they'll typically all show the same public IP, even though each device also has its own separate private IP on the local network.

Does Fixoona log or store my IP address?

No — this page doesn't send your IP anywhere for Fixoona to record; it's simply displayed to you after the lookup service returns it. The browser, screen, and time zone details shown are read directly from your own browser and never leave your device.

Why might this show a different IP than another "what's my IP" site?

If you're using a VPN, proxy, or your ISP just rotated your assigned address, different lookup services can briefly disagree, or you might see a different result minutes apart. This is normal and reflects real, dynamic changes in how you're routed to the internet, not an error.
