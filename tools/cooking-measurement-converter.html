How to Use the Cooking Converter

  1. Enter an amount and choose the unit you're converting from.
  2. Every other volume unit's equivalent appears instantly.
  3. Enter a temperature in either Fahrenheit or Celsius — the other updates automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this account for different ingredient densities, like cups of flour vs. cups of water?

No — this converts pure volume units only (teaspoons, tablespoons, cups, milliliters, and so on), the same way a measuring cup itself works. Converting to a weight measurement like grams would require knowing the specific ingredient's density, which varies a lot between flour, sugar, butter, and liquids.

Are these US or metric cup sizes?

US customary units — a US cup is defined as 236.588 milliliters. Some countries use a slightly different "metric cup" (250 mL), so double-check which your recipe means if it doesn't specify.

How accurate is the oven temperature conversion?

It uses the exact standard formula (°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9), which is mathematically precise — any real-world difference would come from your specific oven's calibration, not the conversion math.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — all conversions happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you enter is ever sent to a server.
