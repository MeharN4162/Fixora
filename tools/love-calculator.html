Frequently Asked Questions

How does this calculator actually work?

It combines the two names you enter and runs them through a simple hashing formula to produce a number between 0 and 100. It's a fun, lighthearted novelty, not a measurement of anything real about a relationship.

Will I get the same result every time with the same two names?

Yes — the result is deterministic, meaning the exact same pair of names will always produce the exact same percentage. There's no randomness involved, just a formula based on the letters in the names.

Does the order I enter the names in matter?

No — the two names are sorted before being combined, so entering "Alex and Sam" gives the exact same result as "Sam and Alex."

Is this based on real relationship science?

No, not at all. This is purely for entertainment — a name-based novelty calculator, not a psychological or scientific compatibility test of any kind.
