How to Convert Currencies

  1. Enter an amount and pick the currency you're converting from.
  2. Pick the currency you want to convert to — the result updates instantly.
  3. Click "Swap Currencies" to flip the two and see the reverse rate.
  4. The line below the result shows the exact exchange rate used and when it was last updated.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often are the exchange rates updated?

Once a day. Rates are pulled from a free, publicly available exchange rate service and cached in your browser for up to 12 hours before checking for a fresh set, so most visits use an already-cached rate rather than making a new request every time.

Are these real-time, live trading rates?

No — these are daily reference rates, not second-by-second interbank trading rates. Actual currency markets move continuously throughout the day, so a rate shown here can differ slightly from what you'd see on a live trading platform at that exact moment.

Why is the rate my bank or card gave me different from what's shown here?

Banks, card networks, and money transfer services typically add their own markup on top of the market exchange rate, plus sometimes a separate foreign transaction fee. The rate shown here is the underlying market reference rate, not what any specific bank or provider will actually charge you.

What happens if the exchange rate service is unavailable?

The tool falls back to the last successfully fetched rates stored in your browser, if any, and shows the timestamp of when those rates were last updated so you know if the data might be a little stale rather than current.
