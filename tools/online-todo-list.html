Online To-Do List

A simple to-do list that saves your tasks right in your browser. No sign-up.

    No tasks yet — add one above.

    How to Use the To-Do List

    1. Type a task and press Enter or click "Add."
    2. Check a task off when it's done.
    3. Click "✕" to delete a task, or "Clear Completed" to remove all finished ones.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Where are my tasks actually stored?

    Your tasks are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device — not on a server or account. That means they'll still be there next time you visit this page in the same browser, but they won't sync to another device or browser.

    Will my list disappear if I clear my browser data?

    Yes — since the list lives in your browser's local storage, clearing your browsing data, using a private/incognito window, or switching browsers will not carry your saved tasks with it.

    Is there a limit to how many tasks I can add?

    There's no hard limit built into this tool, though browsers generally cap local storage at several megabytes per site, which is far more than enough for a practical task list.

    Is my task list ever sent to a server?

    No — everything is stored and managed entirely in your browser using JavaScript and local storage. Nothing you type is ever transmitted anywhere.
