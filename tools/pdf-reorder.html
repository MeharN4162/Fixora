How to Reorder PDF Pages

  1. Upload your PDF — every page renders as a thumbnail labeled with its position and original page number.
  2. Use the up and down buttons on each thumbnail to move it earlier or later in the sequence.
  3. Watch the "Position N (was page M)" label update as you move pages around, confirming the new order.
  4. Click "Save Reordered PDF" and download the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drag and drop pages to reorder them?

No — reordering works through the up and down buttons on each page thumbnail, which move a page one position earlier or later per click. There's no drag-and-drop in this tool.

How do I know what the new page order will be before saving?

Each thumbnail's label shows both its current position and its original page number (e.g. "Position 3 (was page 7)"), so you can confirm the exact order before clicking Save.

Does reordering affect page rotation or content quality?

No — pages are copied directly from the source PDF's structure using pdf-lib rather than being re-rendered, so their rotation, selectable text, and image quality all stay exactly as they were.

Can I move a page to the very top or bottom quickly?

You'll need to click the up or down button repeatedly to move a page across many positions, since each click only shifts it by one — there's no jump-to-top shortcut.

Is there a limit to how many pages I can reorder?

No hard limit, but every page is rendered as a thumbnail image in your browser when the file loads, so very long documents may take longer to load and use more memory since everything runs locally on your device.
