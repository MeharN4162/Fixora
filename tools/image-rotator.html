How to Rotate an Image

  1. Click the upload area or drag in a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Use "Rotate Left" or "Rotate Right" to turn the image 90° at a time, or "180°" to flip it upside down.
  3. Watch the live preview — width and height automatically swap when you rotate to 90° or 270°.
  4. Click "Download Rotated Image" to save the result in the same format as your original upload.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does rotating my image reduce its quality?

Rotating in 90 degree steps doesn't resample or blur any pixels, since the tool simply redraws the canvas at a new angle. However, if you uploaded a JPG or WebP, the download is re-encoded at 92% quality during export, which introduces the same small amount of compression any JPEG/WebP save does.

Why did the width and height swap after I rotated?

Rotating 90 or 270 degrees turns a landscape image into a portrait one (or vice versa), so the canvas dimensions are automatically swapped to match - a 1200x800 photo becomes 800x1200 after a single 90 degree rotation.

What file format is my downloaded image?

The output matches your upload: JPG stays JPG, WebP stays WebP, and everything else (including PNG) is saved as PNG. Transparent PNG or WebP backgrounds are preserved; JPG output gets a white background fill since JPEG can't hold transparency.

Can I rotate by a custom angle, like 15 degrees?

Not with this tool - it's built specifically for quick 90, 180, and 270 degree corrections (the common case for a sideways or upside-down photo). For arbitrary angles you'd need a full image editor.

Is my photo uploaded to a server to be rotated?

No. The rotation happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API's rotate transform - the image file never leaves your device.
