How to Apply Filters to a Photo

  1. Click the upload area or drag in a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Drag the Brightness, Contrast, and Saturation sliders to adjust the look — the preview updates as you move them.
  3. Optionally click a preset pill (Grayscale, Sepia, or Invert) to layer a one-click effect on top of your slider adjustments.
  4. Click "Reset" to return to the original look at any point, or "Download Image" to save your edit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I combine a preset like Sepia with the brightness/contrast sliders?

Yes. Presets and sliders aren't mutually exclusive - the tool builds a single CSS filter string combining your brightness, contrast, and saturation values with whichever preset (grayscale, sepia, or invert) is selected, then applies all of it in one pass.

What do the Brightness, Contrast, and Saturation percentages mean?

100% is the original, unmodified image for each. Values below 100% darken, flatten, or desaturate the image, values above 100% intensify it - the range runs from 0% to 200% on all three sliders.

Does this tool support crop, blur, or other effects?

No - it's focused specifically on brightness, contrast, saturation, and the three one-click presets (grayscale, sepia, invert). For cropping, use Fixora's separate Image Cropper tool.

What format is the downloaded file?

It matches your source: JPG in, JPG out; WebP in, WebP out; anything else (including PNG) exports as PNG. JPG output gets a white background fill since JPEG can't store transparency.

Is the filtering done on a server?

No. All adjustments are applied locally using the Canvas 2D API's built-in filter property, which runs the same brightness/contrast/saturate/grayscale/sepia/invert functions browsers use for CSS - your photo never leaves your device.
