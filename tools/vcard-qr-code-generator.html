vCard QR Code Generator

Generate a scannable QR code so anyone can save your contact details instantly.

How to Use the vCard QR Code Generator

  1. Fill in your name and any contact details you want to share.
  2. The QR code updates instantly.
  3. Download it and add it to a business card, email signature, or badge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when someone scans this?

This QR code encodes your details in the standard vCard format that phones recognize automatically — scanning it prompts an "Add Contact" screen pre-filled with your name, phone, email, and other details, ready to save with one tap.

Which fields are required?

Only a name is required — everything else (phone, email, company, website, address) is optional and only included in the QR code if you fill it in.

Is my information sent anywhere when I generate this?

No — the QR code is generated entirely in your browser, so nothing you enter is ever sent to a server.

Will this work with any phone's camera?

Most modern iPhone and Android camera apps recognize vCard QR codes directly. Older devices may need a separate QR scanner app instead of the native camera.
