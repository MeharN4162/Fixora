How to Calculate Fuel Cost for a Trip

  1. Choose your preferred units — Miles/Gallon or Liters/100km — using the toggle pills.
  2. Enter your trip distance, your vehicle's fuel efficiency, and the price per gallon (or liter).
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the results.
  4. Read the fuel needed for the trip, the total fuel cost, and the cost per mile or kilometer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the calculation differ between MPG and L/100km modes?

In MPG mode, fuel needed is distance divided by MPG, since MPG expresses distance per unit of fuel; in L/100km mode, fuel needed is distance divided by 100 and multiplied by the L/100km figure, since that metric expresses fuel used per 100 km rather than distance per unit of fuel — the tool switches formulas automatically when you toggle units.

Where do I find my vehicle's fuel efficiency number?

Check your car's manual, window sticker for a new vehicle, or manufacturer's spec sheet for its official MPG or L/100km rating — real-world efficiency can vary with driving style, terrain, and vehicle load, so treat the built-in rating as an estimate.

Does this calculator account for fuel price changes during a long trip?

No — it assumes a single, constant price per gallon or liter for the entire trip; if prices vary significantly along your route, the actual total cost may differ from the estimate.

What's the difference between Total Fuel Cost and Cost per Mile?

Total Fuel Cost is the full dollar amount for the whole trip, while Cost per Mile (or per km) divides that total by the distance, which is useful for comparing trip costs on a per-unit-distance basis regardless of total distance.

Can I use this for costs other than gasoline, like diesel or electric charging?

Yes, as long as you enter the correct price per unit and your vehicle's efficiency in the matching unit — the math itself doesn't care what fuel type it represents, it's simply distance, efficiency, and price.
