NATO Phonetic Alphabet Converter

Convert text into the standard NATO spelling alphabet — Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, and so on.

How to Use the NATO Alphabet Converter

  1. Type any text.
  2. Each letter and digit is spelled out using its official NATO word.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NATO phonetic alphabet for?

It's a standardized spelling alphabet (also called the ICAO or international radiotelephony alphabet) used by pilots, the military, and phone support staff to spell out letters clearly over a connection where similar-sounding letters like "B" and "D" could otherwise be confused.

Why these specific words instead of any random words?

Each word was chosen for being maximally distinct from every other word in the alphabet when spoken, minimizing the chance of a letter being misheard — a property that took real testing and refinement to standardize internationally.

Does it handle numbers?

Yes — digits 0 through 9 are spoken using their standard words (Zero, One, Two, and so on) as used in official radiotelephony alongside the letter alphabet.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No — the conversion happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you type is ever sent to a server.
