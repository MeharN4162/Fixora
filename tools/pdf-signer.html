How to Sign a PDF

  1. Upload your PDF — if it has multiple pages, pick which one to sign.
  2. Draw your signature with your mouse or finger, or switch to Upload Image to use a photo of your real signature.
  3. Click anywhere on the page preview to place it, then use the size slider to adjust.
  4. Click Add Signature & Save PDF and download the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a legally binding electronic signature?

No — this places a visual image of your signature onto the PDF page, the same way you'd sign a printed page and scan it back in. It doesn't create a cryptographically verified e-signature with an audit trail, which is what services like DocuSign provide for legal contracts. For a visual signature on personal documents or forms, this works fine; for legally binding contracts, use a dedicated e-signature service.

How do I position my signature exactly where I want it?

Click anywhere on the page preview to place your signature's top-left corner at that spot, then use the size slider to scale it before saving. You can click again to reposition before finalizing.

Can I sign more than one page?

Yes — use the page selector to switch to a different page, place your signature there, then save. Each save applies your current signature and position to the currently selected page.

Should I draw my signature or upload an image of it?

Either works — drawing with your mouse or finger is faster for a one-off signature, while uploading a photo of your actual handwritten signature (on plain paper, cropped tightly) usually looks more natural and is easy to reuse across documents.
