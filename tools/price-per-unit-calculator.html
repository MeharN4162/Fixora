Price Per Unit Calculator

Compare products by price per unit to find the better deal.

How to Compare Unit Prices

  1. Enter each product's name (optional), total price, and size or quantity.
  2. The price per unit for each product is calculated instantly.
  3. The cheapest option is highlighted in green.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do the two products need to use the same unit of measurement?

No — the unit label next to each product's size is just a label for display; the tool simply divides price by size for each product independently, so you can compare a price-per-ounce product against a price-per-count product as long as you understand what each unit means for your decision.

Can I compare more than two products?

Yes — click "Add Product" to add as many as you want to compare at once; the cheapest price per unit is highlighted automatically.

Does this account for quality or bulk-buying tradeoffs?

No — it purely compares price per unit of size. A lower price per unit doesn't automatically mean the best choice if you don't need that much quantity, or if quality genuinely differs between the products being compared.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — every calculation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you enter is ever sent to a server.
