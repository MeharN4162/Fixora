Word Frequency Counter

Find the most frequently used words in any text, with an option to ignore filler words.

How to Use the Word Frequency Counter

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. The top 20 most frequent words appear instantly, ranked by count.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "word" for this tool?

Any run of letters and apostrophes, compared case-insensitively — so "The" and "the" are counted as the same word, and punctuation attached to a word is stripped before counting.

What does the "ignore common words" option do?

It filters out very common filler words like "the," "a," "is," and "and" so the results highlight the more meaningful, topic-specific words in your text instead of words that appear frequently in almost any writing.

What is this useful for?

Spotting overused words in your own writing, getting a quick sense of a document's main topics, or checking keyword usage and repetition for SEO content.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No — the analysis happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, so nothing you paste in is ever sent to a server.
